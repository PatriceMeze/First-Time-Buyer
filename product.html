What's inside

Buying your first home in the UK is one of the biggest financial decisions you'll ever make — and the process can feel deliberately confusing. This handbook strips it back to plain English, walking you through every stage in the order you'll actually face it.

Whether you're just starting to save or you've already had an offer accepted, you'll find a clear, practical chapter waiting for you — with worked examples using real UK figures, scheme comparisons, and the questions to ask at every step.
